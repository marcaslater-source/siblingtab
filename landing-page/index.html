Questions

When does SiblingTab launch?

An early version is open now at app.siblingtab.com. It covers shared expenses with receipt photos, who owes whom, and a spreadsheet export, and there's no charge while it's in early access. More features are on the way; join the waitlist to hear about them.

Is this tax advice?

No. SiblingTab organizes your receipts and records around Canadian tax rules so you or your accountant can file accurately. Always confirm your claims with CRA or a tax professional.

Where is my family's data stored?

In Canada. We'll never sell your data or show you ads.

Do all my siblings need to sign up?

No. One person can start the family tab and invite others whenever they're ready. A monthly summary email, for siblings who'd rather not log in, is available: each person can switch it on in their Account page.

Does it work in Quebec?

Yes for tracking and splitting. Quebec-specific tax credit tagging is on our list.
