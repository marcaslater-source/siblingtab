Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026. Applies to siblingtab.com and the SiblingTab app at app.siblingtab.com.

SiblingTab helps brothers and sisters share the costs of caring for a parent. That means you trust us with money details, receipts and sometimes health information about someone you love. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, who can see it and what you can do about it. It is written to meet Canada's federal privacy law (PIPEDA) and Quebec's privacy law (Law 25).

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your email, your name, and the expenses, receipts and paybacks your family enters.
  • Your family's data is stored in Canada (Montreal).
  • Only the members of a family space can see that family's expenses and receipts.
  • We never sell your data or show ads, and the app has no tracking or analytics tools. Our public website uses Google Analytics to count visits, which you can decline.
  • You can export your data at any time and delete your account yourself.
  • Questions or requests go to hello@siblingtab.com.

1. Who we are

SiblingTab is a small independent service run by its founder, Marc Slater, from Toronto, Ontario, Canada ("SiblingTab", "we", "us"). Marc is responsible for protecting personal information and acts as our privacy officer. You can reach them at hello@siblingtab.com.

2. What we collect

When you visit siblingtab.com or join the waitlist

  • Waitlist and survey: your email address, and anything you choose to add, such as your province and your answers to our short survey.
  • Basic web logs: like every website, our web host records technical details of each visit (IP address, browser type, page requested and time) to keep the site running and secure.
  • Website analytics: if you allow it, Google Analytics records how visitors use siblingtab.com (pages viewed, how you arrived, rough location from your IP address, device and browser type). It is not used in the app, and it never sees your family's data.

When you use the SiblingTab app

  • Your account: your email address and password. Your password is stored in scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody, including us, can read it.
  • Your profile: the name you show to your family and, if you add one, a profile photo.
  • Family spaces: the family name, who is a member, when they joined, and invite links.
  • Expenses: the amount, date, category (for example medication, care home or transport), a short description, who paid, how it is split, and a photo of the receipt if you add one.
  • Paybacks: who paid whom, how much, when, and an optional note.
  • Sign-in records: our database provider keeps records of sign-ins (such as time and IP address) for security.

We do not ask for your bank details, social insurance number, or your parent's health records, and you should not add them (see the next section).

3. Information about other people

SiblingTab is about caring for someone else, so what you enter often describes other people: your parent, your siblings and the businesses you pay. A receipt from a pharmacy or care home can reveal health information about your parent, which is sensitive.

Please only add information you are entitled to share and that your family needs to split costs fairly. If a receipt shows more than the amount and the provider (for example a diagnosis or a health card number), consider covering that part before you take the photo. Your siblings in the same family space will see everything you add there.

4. How we use it

We use your information only to:

  • run the app: log you in, show your family's expenses and balances, and create your exports and tax-time summaries;
  • send emails you need, such as sign-up confirmation and password resets (from noreply@siblingtab.com);
  • if you turn it on in your Account page (it is off unless you do), email you a monthly summary of your own family's expenses, balances and missing receipts, from noreply@siblingtab.com;
  • for waitlist members who ticked the consent box: send the free downloads, tell you about launch, and occasionally ask for feedback, in line with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL);
  • answer your questions and support requests;
  • understand, in aggregate, how people find and use siblingtab.com, so we can improve it;
  • keep the service secure, fix problems, and meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, build marketing profiles, or use your receipts or family data to train artificial intelligence. If we ever want to use your information for a new purpose, we will ask for your consent first.

To run and improve the service, we look at overall totals from our own database, for example how many families use SiblingTab or how many expenses were added this month. These totals never identify you or your family, and no outside analytics company is involved.

By creating an account you consent to the uses described here. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account or contacting us, though without that information we can no longer provide the app to you.

5. Who can see it

Your family. Everyone who has joined a family space can see that family's expenses, receipts, paybacks, member names and profile photos. They cannot see your password or the other family spaces you belong to. Only the person who entered an expense can change or delete it.

Us. We do not browse your family's data. We access it only when you ask us to help, when it is necessary to keep the service secure or working, or when the law requires it.

6. Where it is stored

Your app data, including receipts, is stored in Supabase's Canada (Central) region in Montreal. Some of our providers are based in, or may process information in, other countries such as the United States. Information held outside Canada, or outside Quebec, is subject to the laws of that place and may be accessed by its courts and authorities. Before using a provider, we consider how sensitive the information is and whether the provider protects it adequately.

7. How we protect it

  • All connections use encryption (HTTPS).
  • Database rules check every request, so a person can only read a family's data if they are a member of that family.
  • Receipts and photos are kept in private storage and shown through links that expire after a few minutes.
  • Passwords are hashed, and the app does not use third-party trackers.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach of security creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and report it to the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, and to Quebec's Commission d'accès à l'information where required, as the law requires. You can help by using a strong password you don't use elsewhere and logging out on shared devices.

8. How long we keep it

  • Expenses and receipts: as long as the family space exists. When an expense is deleted, its receipt photo is deleted too.
  • When you delete your account: your login, email address and profile photo are deleted. Any family space where you are the only member is deleted, with all its receipts. In family spaces you share, your siblings keep the expense history and receipts so their balances stay correct, but your name is replaced with "Former member".
  • Backups and logs: copies may remain in our providers' backups and security logs for a limited time before they are overwritten.
  • Waitlist: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

We may keep information longer only where the law requires it.

9. Your rights and choices

You have the right to:

  • See the personal information we hold about you and how it has been used;
  • Correct information that is wrong (you can change your name, photo, email and expenses in the app);
  • Get a copy of your data in a usable format (the app's spreadsheet export does this for expenses);
  • Delete your account at any time in the app under Your account, or ask us to do it;
  • Withdraw consent to emails: every waitlist and monthly summary email has an unsubscribe link, and you can switch the monthly summary off any time in your Account page;
  • Complain. Please contact us first so we can try to fix it. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.

To make a request, email hello@siblingtab.com from the address on your account. We will reply within 30 days. Requests are free.

10. Cookies and browser storage

On siblingtab.com, Google Analytics uses cookies to tell visits apart. We only turn it on if you allow it, and you can change your mind at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of our home page and guides (choosing "No thanks" deletes the Analytics cookies), or by installing Google's opt-out add-on. We do not use advertising cookies, and we have turned off Google's advertising features.

In the app, there are no cookies for tracking or analytics. The app stores your login session and the last family space you opened in your browser's local storage, so you stay signed in. It also saves the app's files on your device so it opens quickly. Logging out removes your session.

11. Age

SiblingTab is for adults. You must be at least 18, or the age of majority where you live, to create an account.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell account holders by email or in the app before it takes effect.
