## Version A: discussion starter (no link in the post)

**Title:** Anyone else the "default" sibling for a parent's care? What helped you?

Lots of people seem to be talking about unequal sibling caregiving this week, and it got me thinking about how it actually happens. In most families I hear about, nobody decides it. One person lives closest or has a flexible job, picks up one favour, and a year later they're doing nearly everything.

A few things I've seen help (from reading, not from a personal story):
- Writing down a normal week of hours and costs, so the others can actually see it
- Making one specific ask with options ("take the pharmacy and insurance paperwork, or put in $X a month?") instead of "you never help"
- A short scheduled family call rather than a group chat
- Long-distance siblings taking on tasks they can do from anywhere: phone calls, booking, paperwork, receipts

If you're in Canada, I put what I've learned into a few plain-English guides (no signup): https://siblingtab.com/guides/

What worked, or didn't, in your family?
